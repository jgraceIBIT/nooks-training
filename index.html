The core tasks in Nooks, each with the rule that matters, the steps, and the mistake to avoid. Every section links to the page in Nooks.

1

Making & logging a call

You can call from the Dialer, the Tasks page, a sequence's Prospects tab, or as a one-off dial.

Key point

The call is the same no matter where you start it — same dialpad, same dispositions, same logging to Salesforce. Pick the path that fits what you're doing.

Which path should I use?
Pick your situation:
Choose a situation above to see the right path.
The paths
  • Dialer — for calling through a list of due call tasks at volume.
  • Tasks page → Start Tasks — one task at a time, so you can read each prospect first. Mixes calls, emails, and LinkedIn.
  • Sequence → Prospects tab — work one sequence's prospects step by step. Good for clearing all of a play's calls at once.
  • One-off dial — for calling one person who has no due task (a callback or referral).
Every call, same finish
  1. Pick the number, click the phone button, have the conversation.
  2. Set a disposition — it auto-saves, no save button.
  3. Hit Log. That's what saves the call to Nooks and Salesforce.
Avoid this

A one-off call does not advance a sequence. If you call someone through a one-off, their sequence task stays open. To work a sequence, call from the Dialer or Tasks page.

Always log the call. If you close the call window without logging, the call is not saved to Nooks or Salesforce, and a log can't be undone.

2

Loading contacts into a sequence

This adds a person to a sequence, which creates your tasks for them. The one thing to get right is which sequence.

Key point

Pick the play that fits the account and enroll them in that play's sequence. You enroll into our existing plays — you don't build your own sequences.

Steps (contact already in Salesforce)
  1. Select the prospect (Prospects list or the account's Prospects tab).
  2. Click Add to sequence.
  3. Pick the right play for the account and confirm.
Our plays
  • VMware Freedom — VMware / Broadcom stack, renewal cost pressure.
  • Shadow AI Security — unsanctioned employee AI use.
  • Agentic Security & Guardrails — securing the AI agents they deploy.
  • Agentic SOC — running / augmenting their SOC.
  • Ransomware Recovery — tested-recovery readiness.
If the prospect is marked NEW (not in Salesforce yet)

People Nooks found on LinkedIn but who aren't in Salesforce yet are tagged NEW. A NEW prospect has to be in Salesforce before you can sequence them. Two ways to get there:

  • From Nooks — Add to CRM: check the NEW prospect → Add to CRM. This only creates the contact in the account. No email or phone comes with it — the record lands blank except for name, title, and LinkedIn URL. You then have to enrich it: open the contact in Salesforce and run Seamless → Find to add email and phone.
  • From LinkedIn — Seamless import: open the prospect's LinkedIn profile → use the Seamless extension to import them into Salesforce. This creates the contact and pulls in email and phone in one step.
  • Then, either way: check the owner in Salesforce (Add to CRM sets it to you, with no auto-routing), then Add to sequence back in Nooks.
Avoid this

Don't expect Add to CRM to fill in contact info — it doesn't. It only puts the contact in the account. The record is blank on email and phone until you enrich it.

Don't use the Nooks "Enrich" button. We enrich with Seamless, which runs in Salesforce and on LinkedIn, not inside Nooks.

3

Sending an email

Nooks logs email activity even when you send from Outlook, but that isn't enough for a sequence email.

Key point

When a sequence gives you an email task, send it from inside Nooks — the task, the sequence's Prospects tab, or the contact's page all work. Sending from Nooks is what marks the step complete and moves the prospect to the next step. What doesn't count is typing the same email in Outlook by hand — the sequence won't know you sent it.

Steps
  1. Open the email from wherever you're working it — your Tasks page, the sequence's Prospects tab, or the contact's page.
  2. Review the drafted copy (edit if you want).
  3. Send from Nooks — the step completes and the sequence advances.
What this affects
  • Sending from Nooks is what completes the step and schedules the next touch.
  • Outlook import is a safety net for one-off emails outside a sequence — not a substitute for working the task.
  • Opens and clicks are tracked either way; advancing the sequence is not.
The sequence's Prospects tab is the fast way to clear one email step for everyone on it at once.
Avoid this

If you see an email task and then send the same email from Outlook by hand, the task stays open. The activity may log, but the step doesn't complete and the prospect doesn't advance — so you'll see an "overdue" task you thought you finished.

4

Finding your tasks & next steps

Everything a sequence asks you to do shows up in one place.

Key point

The Tasks page is your queue — calls, emails, and LinkedIn across every sequence, most overdue first. Overdue means a manual step is waiting on you; nothing advances past a step until you work it.

Where it lives
  • Tasks in the left nav = your full to-do list across every sequence.
  • Call tasks also show up in the Dialer so you can power through them.
  • Sorted most-overdue first — the top of the list is where to start.
Three things you can do to a task
  • Complete it — you did the work; the sequence advances.
  • Skip it — doesn't apply (e.g. LinkedIn message to someone who hasn't accepted). Keeps them in the sequence.
  • Reschedule it — not today; push it out.
Avoid this

Overdue tasks are manual steps waiting on you — work them top to bottom. The list is already sorted most-overdue first, so start at the top. Complete the ones you can do now, skip the ones that don't apply, and reschedule anything you can't get to today.

5

Using AI coaching

Practice your cold calls against AI before you dial real prospects.

Key point

AI Roleplay (Cold Call Training) lets you run a live practice call against an AI prospect built for one of our plays, then review the transcript to see how it went.

Steps
  1. Go to AI Roleplay in the left nav (under Coach).
  2. Pick a bot that matches the play you're working — e.g. VMware Freedom, Ransomware Recovery, Agentic SOC.
  3. Hit Call and run the conversation like a real cold call.
  4. Read the transcript afterward and try again.
What you get
  • A realistic AI prospect tuned to a specific play and persona.
  • Different temperaments to practice against (Friendly, Rude).
  • A transcript of each practice call to learn from.

6

Using AI to build your target lists

This is how you find who to work in Nooks.

Key point

Work from the Accounts page: choose the saved list for the play you're working, open an account, find the right people, and add them to that play's sequence.

The flow
  1. Open Accounts and choose the saved list for the play you're working.
  2. Open an account worth pursuing.
  3. In the Prospects tab, review people — use Find more for coverage.
  4. Or ask the AI Assistant: "who are the likely decision-makers here?"
  5. Select the right people and Add to sequence.
What you get
  • The AI Assistant suggests likely decision-makers at the account.
  • Find more surfaces people not yet in Salesforce.
  • You choose who goes into a sequence.
